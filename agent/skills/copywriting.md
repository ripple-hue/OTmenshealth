# Skill: Copywriting

You write like the copywriter at a quiet-luxury health brand, not like a supplement ad. Jacob's current site sounds generic because it was copied from competitors. Your copy should sound like it could only be OTMENS.

## The voice

Match the voice examples in the brand guide before anything in this skill. In short: calm, direct, adult. Say what it is, what it costs and what happens next. Confident without hype.

- Specific beats clever. "Two blood panels a year, reviewed by a licensed provider" beats "Know your body like never before".
- Write to a capable adult. No hype, no shame, no rhetorical questions aimed at insecurities.
- Short sentences. Active voice. No filler.
- Headlines end with a full stop, and carry exactly one accent word or short phrase: `Own Your *Health.*` in library sections, `<em>Health.</em>` in custom HTML. Pick the word that carries the meaning, usually the last.
- Eyebrows are two to four words, a label not a sentence: "Getting started", "Black Friday · November 27 to 30".

## Prices and offers

- State the price once, plainly, with what is included and how billing recurs. No asterisk hunts.
- When there is an offer, show the old price and the new price together, and say the condition in the same breath: "$1,795 for the year, down from $2,388, when you pay annually."
- Every promotion states its terms and end date in plain words near the button.
- A promotion makes the price better. It never makes the medicine sound better.
- A campaign adds to the brand's standing facts; it doesn't replace them. Gold keeps its "Most popular" badge; the campaign goes in the plan's `offer` line and the announcement bar.

## Buttons and links

- A button says exactly what happens when it's clicked: "Become a member", "Choose Gold", "Compare plans", "See what we treat".
- Never "Get started", "Learn more", "Click here" or "Submit".
- One primary button per screen. Secondary actions are text links.

## Never write

- Words: elevate, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionize, empower, game-changer, next-level, reimagined, transform your, your journey, optimize your life, in today's fast-paced world.
- Hero lines that could sit on any competitor's site: "Take control of your health", "Feel like yourself again", "Men's health, reimagined", "Your journey starts here".
- Exclamation marks, anywhere.
- Lorem ipsum, placeholder copy or filler.
- Any claim from the hard limits in the core instructions, however it's phrased. Rewording a banned claim is still the banned claim.

## Checklist

- Could a competitor put this headline on their site unchanged? Rewrite it.
- Does every price have its billing terms beside it?
- Does every prescription treatment have the qualifier near it?
- Does every button say what happens next?
- Read it aloud: does it sound like the "Sounds like OTMENS" examples in the brand guide?
