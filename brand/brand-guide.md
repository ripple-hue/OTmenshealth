# OTMENS brand guide

Source: Jacob's current site (screenshots, November 2026). Anything not listed here is unknown and must not be invented.

## What OTMENS is

A men's health membership. Labs, providers, prescriptions and supplements in one membership. Every plan starts with bloodwork reviewed by a licensed provider. Available in all 50 states. Clinicians are U.S.-licensed. Joining and scheduling the first blood draw takes under five minutes.

## Facts you may state

**Silver.** $39.99 a month, or $479 for the year. Includes 2 OT functional blood panels a year ($898 value), member pricing on supplements, member pricing on additional diagnostics.

**Gold.** $199 a month, or $1,795 for the year (was $2,388). Three months free when paid annually. Includes a dedicated wellness advisor and health coaching ($5,988 value) and concierge medical care on demand. "Your doctor in your pocket." Gold is the most popular plan.

**Treatment tracks.** Advanced Diagnostics (blood panels, genetic testing, mitochondria test, gut microbiome). Hormone therapy (TRT, enclomiphene). Metabolic (GLP-1 therapy, tirzepatide). Peptides (sermorelin). Longevity (NAD+, supplements). Sexual health (tadalafil).

**Unknown, never state:** medication prices, shipping times, clinician names, review counts, ratings, number of members, number of biomarkers tested, refund terms, insurance.

**Standing qualifier.** Wherever a prescription treatment is named, this must appear nearby: prescription required, a licensed provider decides whether treatment is appropriate, not everyone qualifies.

## Look

Quiet luxury, like an upscale pharmacy brand. Not the dark, aggressive men's-health style. Light pages, with one dark hero on the home page.

- Ink `#17130E`, cream `#F7F1E6`, champagne `#EBDDB8`, honey gold `#D8BC7E`, bronze `#7A5E2C`.
- Headlines in light Helvetica Neue. Exactly one word or short phrase per headline is set in italic gold serif. Mark it with asterisks: `Own Your *Health.*` This is the most recognisable part of the brand. Headlines end with a full stop.
- Small labels (eyebrows) are short, in spaced capitals with a gold dot: "Getting started", "Choose your path".
- Soft rounded cards, a pill-shaped nav, gold buttons, light shadows.
- Membership is shown as metallic Silver and Gold key tags.

## Voice

Calm, direct, adult. Says what it is, what it costs and what happens next. Confident without hype.

**Sounds like OTMENS**
- "Three steps, one plan."
- "Every plan starts with your bloodwork, reviewed by a licensed provider."
- "Retest, compare, and your plan moves with you."
- "Concierge medical care on demand. Your doctor in your pocket."

**Does not sound like OTMENS**
- "Unlock your peak performance today!"
- "Tired of feeling tired? We've got you."
- "The #1 men's health platform."
- "Get started on your journey."

Rules: short sentences, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions aimed at insecurities, one main button per screen, buttons say what happens ("Become a member", "Choose your plan", "Compare plans").

## Images

Products: matte bottles and vials with gold caps and a small "OT" label, on stone blocks, in warm side-light, styled as editorial still life. People: fit men doing endurance sport in moody natural light, never posed, never shirtless.

## Promotions

A promotion changes price or adds convenience. It never changes or strengthens a medical claim. Every promotion states its terms and end date in plain words. The only existing offer is three months free on annual Gold.
