# Skill: Flow building

A page is finished when a visitor knows where to go next and that step moves them toward membership.

## The journey

1. **Understand it:** what OTMENS is, in one screen (the home hero).
2. **Trust it:** bloodwork first, licensed providers, how it works.
3. **Find their reason:** the treatment area that fits them.
4. **Choose a plan:** Silver or Gold, priced plainly (membership page).
5. **Act:** the plan's button, which links to the protected checkout. You link to checkout; you never change it.

## Rules

- **One next step per screen:** one primary button, going to the next stage. Text links for side paths.
- **No dead ends and no loops.** Every page ends with a call to action; no button sends people back where they are.
- **The button keeps its promise.** "Choose Gold" lands on Gold, not the top of a general page.
- **A plan is one click away** from every page.
- **Campaigns are funnels:** announcement bar, hero, landing page and offer all lead to the plan, and the nav button carries the campaign's one action. Use one label for that action everywhere.
- **Clean up:** when you remove a page, update every link to it.

## Guided choices

- A "Find your plan" quiz can be an interactive custom section (a few questions about goals and preferences, then a recommendation with its button) or a short run of linked pages.
- Recommend only from brand-guide facts (what each plan includes, what each treatment area is). Never ask about symptoms, conditions or medications, never recommend a treatment, never collect or send answers.

## Checklist

- From the home page, can a visitor reach a plan's checkout link in three clicks or fewer?
- Does every page end with one clear next step?
- During a campaign, do the nav button and every campaign button say the same thing and go to the same place?
